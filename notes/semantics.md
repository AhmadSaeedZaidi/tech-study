# Useful semantic tags

More at [W3Schools](https://www.w3schools.com/html/html5_semantic_elements.asp).

- `header` — top area of a page or section, usually contains the site title or intro.
- `nav` — groups the main navigation links.
- `main` — the page's primary unique content (one per page).
- `section` — a thematic grouping of content, like a banner or signup block.
- `article` — a self-contained composition, such as a blog post or news item.
- `aside` — tangential content related to the main content (sidebars, pull-quotes).
- `figure` / `figcaption` — groups media (images, diagrams) with an optional caption.
- `footer` — bottom area of a page or section, often contains copyright and contact info.
- `address` — contact information for the author or site (typically placed in a footer).

Tip: use these elements to improve document structure, accessibility, and semantics.
